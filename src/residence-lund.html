Residence Lund

Residential · Gothenburg, Sweden · 2019

Category Residential
Location Gothenburg, Sweden
Year 2019
Size 310 m²
Services Interior Design
About The Project

A home rebuilt around how it is actually lived in.

Residence Lund is a complete interior transformation of a 1970s villa in the Askim district of Gothenburg. The clients — a family of four with a serious art collection and a serious requirement for storage — came to us after two previous architects had failed to reconcile the two.

Our solution was structural rather than decorative. We removed three internal walls, reorganised the ground floor around a central gallery corridor that doubles as the home's main circulation route, and designed a custom millwork system that runs the full length of the upper floor, housing the collection, the books, and everything else without ever looking like mere storage.

Lead Architect Jonas Rein
