Services Interior Design
About The Project

Where work feels worth doing.

Atelier Bergmann is the Stockholm headquarters of a Scandinavian fashion house that asked us a question we had not been asked before — can you make an office that people are reluctant to leave? We took the question seriously.

The result is a 1,100 square metre workspace spread across two floors of a 1920s office building in Östermalm. The layout is deliberately non-hierarchical — no corner offices, no glass boxes for management, and no generic open plan either. Instead, a series of varied rooms with distinct qualities of light allow people to choose how and where they work throughout the day.
