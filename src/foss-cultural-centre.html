Foss Cultural Centre

Cultural · Oslo, Norway · 2021

Category Cultural
Location Oslo, Norway
Year 2021
Size 8,400 m²
Services Architecture
About The Project

A civic building that earns its place.

The Foss Cultural Centre was commissioned by the City of Oslo as a new home for the performing arts in the Grünerløkka district. The programme is ambitious — a concert hall, black box theatre, rehearsal spaces, public library, and a ground floor that remains open to the neighbourhood at all hours.

Our response to the brief was a building that refuses to be monumental. The facade is clad in locally sourced granite that weathers over time, matching the surrounding nineteenth-century streetscape in material if not in form. Inside, the spaces are generous without being grand — designed for use rather than for photography, though they photograph well regardless.

Lead Architect Erik Halvorsen
Interior Design Jonas Rein
