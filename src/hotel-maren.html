Hotel Maren

Hospitality · Bergen, Norway · 2022

Category Hospitality
Location Bergen, Norway
Year 2022
Size 3,200 m²
Services Architecture & Interior
About The Project

Hospitality as a form of restraint.

Hotel Maren occupies a converted warehouse on Bergen's historic Bryggen waterfront. The building's industrial bones — exposed timber trusses, raw brick, and original cast iron columns — were preserved and used as the foundation for a 42-room boutique hotel that feels simultaneously old and completely new.

The brief asked for warmth without sentimentality. We responded with a palette of smoked oak, brushed bronze, and deep wool textiles that absorb rather than reflect light. Every room faces the fjord. Every corridor ends in a window. The building knows where it is and is not embarrassed about it.

Lead Architect Jonas Rein
Interior Design Marta Solberg
