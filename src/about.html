Seventeen years of considered spaces.

We are a full-service architecture and interior design studio based in Oslo. We work with private clients, developers, and cultural institutions across Scandinavia and Northern Europe.

Our Philosophy

Good design doesn't announce itself.

We started Halcyon because we were tired of spaces that performed. Lobbies designed to impress rather than welcome. Homes styled for photographs rather than for life. Offices built around a brief rather than around the people who would spend their days inside them.

Good design is often almost invisible. It is the proportion of a window, the weight of a door, and the temperature of a material under your hand. We do not have a signature style. We have a signature standard.
