Our Philosophy

We don't decorate spaces.
We define them.

Halcyon Studio was founded in Oslo in 2015 on a single conviction that good design is not ornamental. It is structural and it changes how people move and think inside a space. Sixteen years later that conviction has not changed. The budgets have.
