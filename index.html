Our Philosophy

We don't decorate spaces.
We define them.

Halcyon Studio was founded in Oslo in 2009 on a simple conviction: good design is not ornamental. It is structural, material, and deeply tied to how people move through a place. Seventeen years later, that conviction still guides every commission.
